## Change

- One pure, schema/unit-based conjunction compiler; no customer-question, product, category, brand or SKU exceptions.
- The existing server-owned adjustable-range proof still owns only its current relationship.
- Each other atomic measured obligation must independently compile through existing customer-authority, unit and live-facet rules. An unprojectable second property cannot disappear behind the first successful projection.
- A customer-owned exact constraint may entail a weaker model bound only if the complete live enum family of the stronger constraint is a subset of the desired bound on the same exact facet. Keep the stronger customer constraint; do not replace it with the model threshold. No rounding 380→400, no nominal 6 A inferred from a 6 A load, no supply/insulation substitution.
- Complete enum families are bounded to 12. A larger facet is allowed when its actual satisfying family is complete and small. An over-limit family cannot be truncated to fabricate an implication.
- Every retained criterion is frozen into both retrieval and final card gates. Passing search membership is still insufficient SKU evidence.
- Symbolic/unresolved obligations, compound multi-property clauses and unproved independent exact facet declarations stay closed. Supported scalar/range clauses retain their directions and scope.

## Activation and limits

The dimension/schema algorithm is reusable, with tests beyond voltage. Rollout is intentionally limited to the existing structured measured-declaration path with a server certificate. It does not change exact-marking search, ordinary broad assortment, replacement, paired-fit, room-total reasoning or specification-manager handoff. General customer authority is changed only for an explicitly per-product operating supply, not system totals or an unknown scope.

This is not a promise of automatic engineering design for every product. If live characteristics cannot prove the operating point, the bot must still refuse or hand off rather than infer compatibility. The new HTTP/SSE positive fixture uses synthetic 380 V product evidence; this does not establish real catalog availability or the outcome of a fresh paid model response.
